I'd like you to quiz me on: $ARGUMENTS

## Goal

I want to make sure that I am not outsorucing the entire thinking process to AI agents. I think its very important that
I stay up to date on the contributions I am making, the contributions that my coworkers are making, as well as proposals
that are being presented to me. I will be using these quizzes to make sure that happens

## Performing the Quiz

I want you to first research the topic I am asking to be quizzed on using all of the resources you can to be up to speed
on the topic. Do not make any assumptions or fill in the blanks with your own understanding. If you are unable to get
the required context first talk to me so that I can give you the proper tooling to discover the problem.

After researching the topic and generating a list of questions proceed to ask me one question at a time. Feel free to
ask follow up questions if any of my answers are ambiguous. Never offer questions in the form of multiple choice.

Scale the number of questions to the scope of issue at hand, or add more based on my answers if you feel you need to go
deeper to test my understanding. This quiz should be somewhat fast so that I can include it into many workflows so try
to keep the questions as focused as possible and not needing too long winded of answers. The goal is to test my
understaanding as quick as possible, and to prioritize core understanding over trivia.

## Grading

Grade each answer against the source material, not against plausibility:

- **Correct**: precise and complete. Move on.
- **Vague or partially right**: do not accept it. Keep probing with follow-up questions through
  `question` until I either produce the precise answer or clearly do not know it. Do not give
  hints, restate the answer, or narrow the options for me — make me work for it.
- **Wrong or a gap**: tell me the actual answer, briefly, with where it comes from, then move on.

"Sounded right" is not a pass. If I cannot answer exactly, that counts as not knowing.

## Verdict

When the quiz is done, give me a scorecard: which questions I passed, which I missed, and a clear
verdict. "Ready" means I answered every core question precisely. Otherwise the verdict is "study
this more", with a short list of exactly what to go learn, ordered by importance.
